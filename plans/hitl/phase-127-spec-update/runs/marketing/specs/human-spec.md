Atlas AI Consulting is a website designed for technology-curious CEOs evaluating AI consulting firms, providing expert AI strategy, development, and training services. Its primary goal is to guide potential partners toward transformative AI solutions. The site’s aesthetic uses a dark background of `#0A1128` for primary elements and `#151D38` for secondary, with `#FFFFFF` for main text and `#A0A7B4` for secondary. A bold `#007BFF` serves as the primary accent, complemented by `#3399FF`. The site features sections for a hero, navigation, features, pricing, testimonials, logos, and contact. Note: theme.mode declared light but palette luminance is closer to dark.

The site opens with a hero section, immediately presenting the headline "Elevate Your Business with AI" and prompting visitors to "Book a Discovery Call." A fixed navigation bar allows easy access to different sections. Following the hero, a features section uses a three-column article grid to detail key AI services. Social proof is built through a logos section displaying partner companies. The site dedicates two distinct pricing sections, both using a 3-tier variant with "price-card" components, enabling visitors to compare service packages. Testimonials are displayed in a grid layout, showcasing client successes. The site employs the Inter font family for both headings and body text, with primary accents highlighting interactive elements and calls to action like "Book a Discovery Call."

This site differentiates itself from a platform like Toptal by explicitly detailing tiered service packages for AI consulting rather than individual contractor profiles. The design aims to achieve a measurable win condition: visitors will click "Book a Discovery Call" at least once within five minutes of exploring service packages, indicating a strong interest in Atlas AI's offerings.
