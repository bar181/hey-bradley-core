Bradley Ross | Visual Designer is a portfolio site showcasing visual design work, specifically tailored for agency creative directors seeking freelance visual designers for upcoming projects. The site uses a light theme with a primary background of `#fdfaf6` and text in `#111`, accented by `#80a490`. Key sections include a navigation menu, hero, video reel, project grid, and contact information. The site's tagline is "Visual designer portfolio for Bradley Ross".

The site opens with a simple navigation menu featuring "Bradley Ross" and essential links. A centered hero section immediately introduces "Bradley Ross, Visual Designer" with the headline "Crafting thoughtful visual experiences." and a "View Projects" call to action. Following this, a dedicated video reel section (`video-01`) presents a curated demonstration of design work. The main content is a project grid displaying six `project-card` components in a `columns` section (`projects-01`). The site concludes with a simple contact section (`contact-01`) providing direct links for inquiries. The typography pairs the Outfit font for headings with Inter for body text, creating a modern, clean aesthetic.

Unlike a Behance portfolio, this site offers a focused, dedicated experience that emphasizes direct client engagement. A prospective client will click "View Projects" within their first visit, then proceed to click one of the contact links to inquire about a project, demonstrating clear interest in collaboration.
