Hey Bradley is a personal site crafted for the aspiring creator early in their journey, particularly those facing creative blocks and seeking inspiration. The site helps visitors transform their ideas into reality through intuitive storytelling and AI-driven insights. It features a dark theme with a `bgPrimary` of `#1a0000` and `bgSecondary` of `#330000`, accented by `#cc3333` and `#ff6666`. The core sections include a Hero, Navigation, Articles, Author Bio, and a Newsletter signup. The site has no tagline.

The visitor experience begins with a `navbar-01` menu featuring the "Hey Bradley" brand, providing navigation to other sections. Immediately following is the `hero-01` section, a prominent, centered display titled "Your ideas, made real." and "The whiteboard that listens," designed to capture attention and prompt exploration. This is followed by `articles-01`, an article grid showcasing featured content. Further down is the `author-bio-01` hero, a left-aligned introduction to the person behind the site, building trust. The site concludes with an `newsletter-01` inline subscription form, encouraging continued engagement. The typography pairs the "Cormorant Garamond" heading font with "Inter" for body text, maintaining a cohesive aesthetic. The accent color `#cc3333` highlights interactive elements and key information.

Unlike Medium, which primarily serves as a platform for publishing completed work, Hey Bradley actively aids the ideation process for personal projects. This site provides tools and inspiration specifically tailored to creators struggling to begin. The win condition for Hey Bradley is for visitors to click the "Read the Stories" button, signifying their engagement with new ideas, on their first visit, within minutes of landing on the homepage.
