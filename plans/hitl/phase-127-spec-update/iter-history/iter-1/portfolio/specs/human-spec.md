This site is the online portfolio for Bradley Ross, a visual designer. Its primary purpose is to showcase his work and demonstrate his capability in crafting thoughtful visual experiences. The site is designed for potential clients and collaborators who are seeking a visual designer, aiming to provide them with a clear understanding of his design aesthetic and skill set.

The site is structured to guide visitors through Bradley's work. It begins with a 'navbar-01' featuring "Bradley Ross" and links, followed by a 'hero-01' section that introduces "Bradley Ross, Visual Designer" with the tagline "Crafting thoughtful visual experiences." A 'video-01' section immediately after presents a design reel, visually demonstrating his style. The core of the portfolio is the 'projects-01' section, a project-grid displaying six individual project cards. Finally, a 'contact-01' section provides links for connecting. The site uses a light mode theme with a palette featuring '#fdfaf6' for the primary background and '#80a490' as an accent, with typography set in 'Inter' for body text and 'Outfit' for headings.

The site is notable for its direct, clean presentation of a visual designer's work. Success will be measured by how effectively it conveys Bradley Ross's design capabilities, leading visitors to understand his talent and prompting them to initiate contact for potential projects. The clear focus on a video reel and project grid ensures that the visual work itself is front and center, allowing the design to speak for itself.
