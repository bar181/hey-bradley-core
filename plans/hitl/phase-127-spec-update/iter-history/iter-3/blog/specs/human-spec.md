Hey Bradley is a platform designed to transform raw ideas into structured narratives, using AI to guide users through the storytelling process. It's built for individuals interested in AI, narrative design, and creative technology, serving as a hub for content and philosophical insights. The site aims to engage visitors with its philosophy, showcase its capabilities, and encourage newsletter subscriptions, rather than focusing on direct product sales or extensive technical documentation.

The site is structured with a dark theme, utilizing a palette of deep reds like `#1a0000` for backgrounds and `#cc3333` for accents, with `Inter` for body text and `Cormorant Garamond` for headings. The user journey begins with a compelling hero section at the top, immediately engaging visitors with a badge, heading such as "Your ideas, made real.", and a call-to-action. A global navigation menu, simply labeled "Hey Bradley" with links, provides persistent access to other areas. Below the hero, a three-column layout of article cards, like those in the `articles-01` section, displays curated content. Further down, an author introduction, `author-bio-01`, presents the creator, and a final inline newsletter section, `newsletter-01`, invites email subscriptions.

The site’s success is measured by visitor engagement with content, their understanding of the product’s core philosophy, and ultimately, growth in newsletter subscriptions. It offers a focused experience, presenting a clear vision for AI-assisted narrative development through thoughtfully arranged content and a cohesive visual identity. The emphasis is on building an interested community around the principles of AI-driven creative assistance.
