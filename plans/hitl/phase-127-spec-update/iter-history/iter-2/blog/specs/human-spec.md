Hey Bradley is a personal content platform designed to make complex ideas and insights accessible to a general web audience. It serves as a digital whiteboard, presenting Bradley's thoughts and perspectives in an engaging, readable format. The core purpose is to provide an online space where visitors can discover compelling information and interact with the content, fostering a deeper understanding of the presented material.

The site follows a dark theme, utilizing a palette of deep reds and off-whites with `#1a0000` as the primary background and `#cc3333` for accents. Typography relies on `Inter` for body text and `Cormorant Garamond` for headings. A global navigation menu, identified by "Hey Bradley," provides persistent links. The initial `hero-01` section grabs attention with a badge, heading, text, and call-to-action, framing the site's value. Following this, an `articles-01` section presents three article cards in a column layout. Further down, an `author-bio-01` section introduces the author with an image and text. Finally, a `newsletter-01` section allows users to subscribe via an inline form.

The success of Hey Bradley will be measured by its ability to engage visitors with insightful content and encourage interaction. Its simple, focused structure and dark aesthetic aim to create an environment where the ideas themselves take center stage. The site avoids complex real-time collaboration or e-commerce, instead prioritizing clear, accessible presentation of thought.
