Atlas AI Consulting is a professional services site designed for a VP of Engineering at a mid-market enterprise seeking external partners for AI transformation. The site, titled "Atlas AI Consulting," carries the tagline "Expert AI consulting for strategic implementation, development, and training to transform your business." Its palette features a dark primary background of `#0A1128` with accent colors `#007BFF` and `#3399FF`. Key sections include an AI consulting overview, service delineation, client trust indicators, and success stories.
Note: theme.mode declared `light` but palette luminance is closer to `dark`.

The site begins with a centered hero section (#hero-01) introducing the value proposition with a heading "Elevate Your Business with AI," and features a prominent call to action. A simple navigation menu (#navbar-01) with the brand "Atlas AI Consulting" is available site-wide for easy access. Following the hero, a columns component (#features-01) delineates core service offerings, and two logos sections (#logos-01) display client trust indicators. Two pricing components (#pricing-01) showcase tiered service packages, and a testimonials grid (#testimonials-01) provides social proof. The typography throughout utilizes the `Inter` font family for both headings and body text, with the accent colors providing visual emphasis. Two identical contact sections (#contact-01, #contact-02) close the site, each inviting visitors to "Book a Discovery Call."

Unlike Toptal, Atlas AI Consulting offers clear pricing and dedicated service packages for focused AI strategic implementation, development, and training. The site aims for a visitor to click "Book a Discovery Call" at least once per session, demonstrating engagement with the primary conversion goal.
