Hey Bradley is a personal site for independent creators seeking inspiration and tools to articulate complex ideas. The site operates in a dark mode, using a rich `#1a0000` primary background with `#fefefe` text, accented by `#cc3333`. There is no declared tagline for the site. Key sections include a primary hero, articles, an author bio, and a newsletter subscription. The `navbar-01` navigation is rendered first, before the main content, allowing immediate access to site navigation.
Note: theme.mode declared `dark` but palette luminance is closer to `dark`.

The `navbar-01` menu section leads the site, simply presenting the "Hey Bradley" title alongside three navigation links. Following this is the `hero-01` section, a centered hero featuring a badge and text that culminates in three headings, "Your ideas, made real.", "The whiteboard that listens", and "Unlocking the story behind your next big thing." This section includes the primary CTA button. Next, the `articles-01` section displays a grid of three article cards for content discovery. The site concludes with an `author-bio-01` section, a left-aligned hero introducing the site's creator with an image, and finally, an `newsletter-01` section with an inline subscribe form. The typography pairs the elegant Cormorant Garamond for headings with the accessible Inter for body text, creating a balanced and readable experience, all highlighted by the `#cc3333` accent.

Unlike Medium, Hey Bradley specifically curates content and tools designed to help creators develop their ideas from concept to execution. A visitor will book their next step by clicking the "Your ideas, made real." hero button within 30 seconds of their first visit.
